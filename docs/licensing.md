# Licensing & provenance boundary

This repo does **not** ship a top-level open-source license, on purpose. It mixes
original work with material adapted from third parties whose license terms have
not been fully verified. Adding a broad license (MIT/Apache/etc.) over that
material would misrepresent rights we do not clearly hold. Until provenance is
resolved, treat this repository as **"source-available, all rights reserved,"**
published for reference rather than redistribution.

## Authorship and known provenance

Stated conservatively — some files mix original work with conventional dotfiles
idioms, generated output, and vendor-documented commands:

- The shell scripts (`setup.sh`, `setup-linux.sh`, `clone-repos.sh`,
  `harden-vps.sh`, `setup-obsidian-sync.sh`, `dot`, `scripts/`) were written for
  this setup, but they invoke third-party installers and reuse common patterns;
  the install commands and snippets they wrap belong to their upstreams.
- `.p10k.zsh` is generated by Powerlevel10k's `p10k configure` and carries that
  project's upstream notices inline (MIT upstream).
- `.zshrc` derives from the Oh My Zsh template with local edits; Oh My Zsh is
  MIT-licensed upstream.
- `.tmux.conf`, `.gitconfig`, and files under `config/` are small,
  hand-maintained, mostly conventional settings. No broad ownership claim is
  made over individual idiomatic lines.

Where authorship is uncertain, assume it may not be exclusively ours and check
before reusing.

## Adapted third-party material (provenance to confirm)

- **`templates/pi/` (opt-in Pi scaffold)** and several files under
  `templates/pi/agent/skills/` are **adapted from
  [dmmulroy/.dotfiles](https://github.com/dmmulroy/dotfiles)**. At the time of
  writing that upstream had no clear license grant covering reuse. The scaffold
  was reworked (rewritten READMes, placeholder configs, private endpoints and
  tokens removed), but the derivation means its licensing is **unresolved**.
- Skill/prompt content under `agent/skills/` may further derive from other
  upstreams (e.g. engineering-practice guides). Confirm each before reuse.

## Installed-but-not-vendored software

Setup scripts install third-party tools (Homebrew formulae/casks, npm packages,
Oh My Zsh, Powerlevel10k, Herdr, Hunk, Tailscale, etc.). Those retain their own
upstream licenses; this repo only automates fetching them and vendors no lockfiles
for them.

## If you want to reuse part of this repo

1. Identify whether the part is original (above) or adapted.
2. For adapted material, check the upstream repo's current license and, where it
   is unclear, ask the upstream author for permission.
3. Prefer forking and adapting for your own machine over redistribution.
